#1 Ready
What it is
Your daily go/no-go meter. Combines four inputs into one number — are conditions good enough to act today?
70+ = clean, act on signals
45–69 = caution, reduce size
below 45 = wait, protect capital
What makes up the math
Setup quality (qualified names available) + market risk (inverted) + top signal strength + available capital headroom. Pulled from your ACTION_ENGINE tab.

#2 Risk
What it is
How dangerous the current market environment is. Lower = safer to deploy capital.
below 35 = green light
35–60 = yellow, watch size
60+ = red, protect first
What makes up the math
VIX level + % of holdings in SELL territory + macro stress signals + concentration risk score. High VIX alone can push this above 60.

#3 Plan
What it is
The system's suggested playbook for today. Not a command — your MODE pill overrides this.
LEAN = fewer positions, tighter sizes
AGG = more exposure allowed
How it decides
If READY ≥ 70 AND RISK ≤ 40 AND ≥ 3 qualified names → suggests AGG. Otherwise suggests LEAN. Your ENGINE tab can hardcode this.

#4 Mode
What it is
Your manual override of the system's plan. Flip this when your gut differs from the model.
LEAN → base size $50–100, max 30% per theme
AGG → base size $75–150, max 50% per theme
Effect on the system
Changes conviction sizing formula, radar graduation speed (AGG = 1 fewer session required), and LCS thresholds used in Compare Mode.
manual switch
#5 Min Score
What it is
The gate score. No ticker gets a BUY signal unless it clears this number.
≥ 75 = qualified, can buy
70–74 = watch zone
50–69 = trim or hold
below 50 = sell signal
What the score measures
Composite of Markov state, momentum quality, sector fit, relative strength, and continuation odds — all weighted and normalized to 0–100 in your RANKING_EXPORT tab.

What it is
Total market value of all positions in your HOLDINGS tab. Excludes SGOV safety pocket (tracked separately).
Sum of (Shares × Current Price)
across all active holdings
How it's used
Drives the portfolio donut weights, theme concentration %, stress map node sizing, and the conviction-based position sizing formula.

#7 Confidence
What it is
Portfolio-level confidence index — one number summarizing how much the system trusts the whole portfolio right now. Weighted average of every holding's LCS score adjusted for concentration risk.
80+ = high confidence across the board
60–79 = mixed — some names weak
below 60 = system is cautious overall
What makes it up
Average LCS across all holdings, weighted by position size, minus a penalty for theme concentration above 30%.
